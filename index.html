Common questions

How do I back up my notes?

In the sidebar, tap the gear button, then Export All Notes. Save the backup file somewhere safe, like iCloud Drive in the Files app. To restore, tap the gear, then Import Notes and choose your backup. Importing only adds pages you don't already have; it never deletes anything.

I deleted Ferrule. Can I get my notes back?

Only from a backup. Your notes live on your iPad, so deleting the app deletes them too. We recommend exporting a backup now and then.

Why doesn't my finger draw?

By default, only Apple Pencil writes and your fingers scroll, so your palm never leaves stray marks. To draw with a finger, open the tool panel (the round button in the corner) and turn on Finger Drawing.

What gestures are there?

  • Two-finger tap: undo
  • Three-finger tap: redo
  • Three-finger swipe: turn to the next or previous page. Swipe past the last page to start a new one.
  • Pinch: zoom in on a page
  • Apple Pencil double-tap or squeeze: follows your choice in iPad Settings → Apple Pencil

How do I add and edit pictures?

Open the tool panel and choose Photos, Camera, Files or Paste under Insert Picture, or drag a picture in from another app. Tap a picture with your finger to select it, then drag, resize or rotate it with its handles. The toolbar at the top has crop, colors, borders, layering and more.

How do I write on a PDF?

Inside a notebook, tap the Import PDF button in the sidebar and pick a PDF from Files. It becomes a page you can write on. Use Share → Share as PDF to send it with your writing included.

The Camera button is missing.

The camera option only appears on iPads with a camera. If you said no when Ferrule asked for camera access, you can turn it back on in iPad Settings → Ferrule.

Does Ferrule collect my data?

No. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and your notes stay on your iPad. See the Privacy Policy.

Ferrule Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

The short version: Ferrule doesn't collect, store or share any of your personal information. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your notes stay on your iPad.

Your notes stay on your iPad

Everything you create in Ferrule (notebooks, pages, handwriting, imported PDFs and pictures) is saved only in the app's storage on your iPad. It isn't uploaded to any server, and we (the developer) can't see it. It's included in your iPad's own iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on, which Apple manages under its own privacy policy.

Information we collect

None. Ferrule has no sign-in, contains no advertising or analytics tools, and doesn't connect to the internet to send information anywhere.

Features that use your iPad's capabilities

  • Photos and Files: when you choose a picture or PDF to add, Ferrule copies only that item into the page. It can't see the rest of your library or files.
  • Camera: only used when you tap Camera to take a picture for a page. The photo is saved only on that page. You can turn camera access off at any time in iPad Settings → Ferrule.
  • Convert to Text and Solve Math: handwriting is read entirely on your iPad using Apple's built-in text recognition. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Sharing and backups

When you share a page as a PDF or image, or export a backup, Ferrule creates a file and hands it to the place you choose, such as Files, Mail or Messages. What happens after that is up to you and the app or service you send it to.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPad's settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't include your notes, and they help us fix bugs. You can change this in iPad Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. TestFlight beta testers may also choose to send us feedback and screenshots through TestFlight.

Children

Ferrule doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
